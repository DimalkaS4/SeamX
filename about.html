About SeamX

Our Mission

SeamX is a dynamic team of five undergraduate engineers from the Department of Textile & Apparel Engineering at the University of Moratuwa. We are committed to revolutionizing the textile industry through innovative research, sustainable practices, and cutting-edge technology. Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic excellence and real-world industrial applications, creating solutions that are both environmentally responsible and economically viable.

Our Story

Founded in 2024, SeamX emerged from a shared passion for textile innovation and sustainable manufacturing. What started as a group of like-minded engineering students collaborating on academic projects has evolved into a comprehensive initiative focused on pushing the boundaries of textile technology.

Our team brings together diverse expertise spanning digital design, business analytics, chemical processing, quality assurance, and technical documentation. This multidisciplinary approach enables us to tackle complex challenges in the textile industry from multiple angles, ensuring comprehensive and innovative solutions.

Through our entrepreneurial venture "STUD Designs," we're not just theorizing about the future of textiles—we're actively building it, one innovative product at a time.

What We Do

Research & Development

We conduct cutting-edge research in biomaterials, sustainable textiles, and innovative manufacturing processes. Our work on spider silk biopolymers and natural dye valorization demonstrates our commitment to next-generation materials.

Entrepreneurship

Through STUD Designs, we transform academic knowledge into market-ready products. We design, develop, and market high-quality apparel that combines style with sustainable manufacturing practices.

Digital Innovation

We leverage digital tools for design, prototyping, and marketing. From 3D virtual fitting to comprehensive web development, we bridge traditional textile engineering with modern technology.

Quality Assurance

We implement rigorous quality control frameworks and testing procedures to ensure that every product and process meets the highest industry standards for performance and reliability.

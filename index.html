Our Collective Vision

As **SeamX**, our collective goal is to bridge the gap between engineering theory and practical application in the textile and apparel sector. We leverage our diverse skills—from digital design and analytics to chemistry and manufacturing strategy—to develop solutions that are efficient, sustainable, and forward-looking. We are proud to represent the Department of Textile & Apparel Engineering at the University of Moratuwa.
